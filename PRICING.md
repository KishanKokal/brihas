---

## 2. What Is a "Credit"?

A credit is our unit for measuring AI usage. Every character you send to the AI (**input**) and every character the AI sends back (**output**) draws down your credit balance.

Input and output characters aren't consumed at the same rate — generating a response (output) uses credits faster than simply reading a prompt (input):

| | Credits consumed |
|---|---|
| Per 1,000 input characters | ~0.75 credits |
| Per 1,000 output characters | ~3.75 credits (5x faster than input) |

**In plain terms, per seat (30,000 credits/month):**

| Scenario | Characters available |
|---|---|
| If used entirely for **input** | ~40,000,000 characters (40M) |
| If used entirely for **output** | ~8,000,000 characters (8M) |
| Typical mixed usage | Somewhere between the two, depending on your input:output ratio |

**Rule of thumb:** 1 credit ≈ 1,333 input characters, or ≈ 267 output characters.

---

## 3. Important: Seats ≠ Number of Users

The number of seats you purchase is **not** the number of people who can use the product.

- A "seat" simply represents a **block of 30,000 monthly credits** added to your organization's shared pool.
- **Any number of people** (n users) within your organization can use the product — the seat count has no bearing on headcount.
- Seats only determine **how many total credits your organization has available each month**, which is then drawn upon collectively by all your users.

So the right way to think about seats is: *"How much total AI usage capacity does my organization need?"* — not *"How many employees will log in?"*

## 4. Recommended Number of Seats

Based on your usage over the last 2 months of trial, 3P has averaged:

> **~400,000 credits / month**

Since each seat provides 30,000 credits:

```
400,000 credits ÷ 30,000 credits/seat ≈ 13.3 seats
```

### Recommended: 13 Seats

- Base credits: 13 × 30,000 = 390,000/month
- Monthly cost: 13 × ₹11,999 = **₹1,55,987**
- This covers the bulk of your average usage (390,000 of the 400,000 credits typically consumed).
- Seat count can be adjusted in future billing cycles based on actual consumption trends.

---

## 5. Overage Policy

If 3P exhausts all available credits before the billing cycle ends, additional credits can be purchased at:

> **₹0.50 per credit**

This ensures there's never a hard stop on usage — teams can keep working, and any extra consumption is simply billed at this per-credit rate.
